Wrapper Types

A Text that behaves like a string — forwarding members, operators and printing to what it wraps, and built straight from a literal — plus a Settings with full control of every read and write. Shows Delegate<T> resolved by the typechecker (so a typo and a bad argument are still compile errors), OpIndex / OpNewIndex as Saule’s __index / __newindex, and why OpFrom<T> converts at some sites and not others.
